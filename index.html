Personal productivity tool

Daily Hub

Daily Hub is a private planning dashboard that runs on its owner's own computer. Each morning it reads the owner's calendars and email and lays out the day: meetings, a suggested task list, and things that still need a reply.

Who it's for

Daily Hub is built and used by one person, Colin Mitchell, for his own accounts. It isn't offered to the public, and there's nothing to sign up for.

What it does with Google accounts

  • Gmail (read-only): reads recent messages to build the day's plan and to suggest inbox cleanup.
  • Google Calendar (read-only): reads events to show the day, week and month.
  • Inbox cleanup (separate, optional permission): applies a label, archives, or moves to Trash only the messages the owner has reviewed and approved in a batch. It never sends email and never permanently deletes anything.

Everything is stored on the owner's computer. There is no Daily Hub server and no account database.
